Changes: retained only amenity=place_of_worship + religion=muslim; excluded known access=private/no and locations outside the app's Malaysia boundary polygons; selected/truncated listing fields and rounded coordinates. Building/area locations use an internal representative point; line features use a midpoint. Geographic filtering reuses geoBoundaries gbOpen MYS ADM1 (2017), OSM-derived, rounded/grouped into the app's regions. Some border/island places may be omitted by this approximation.
Boundary source: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/main/releaseData/gbOpen/MYS/ADM1

Coverage: Malaysia, including Peninsular and East Malaysia. Only mapped and appropriately tagged places are included; this is not an exhaustive or verified mosque register. Public access, opening hours, Friday prayer availability and facilities are not independently verified. Coordinates may represent a building rather than a routable entrance. Check road access and the entrance before travelling.

Derived database available under ODbL 1.0 at /prayer-places.json on this same site. Extraction code is scripts/extract-prayer-places.py. No paid API calls, per-visitor public Overpass queries, GPS trails or journey history are added by this dataset.

Local naming corrections: selected missing names/addresses and aliases may be enriched from the existing licensed Overture directory and verified official listings. Coordinates remain the original OSM points. See data/prayer-place-overrides.json for evidence and checks.
