# Automatic route toll estimates — initial coverage

Class 1 passenger cars only. Every amount is a **partial known subtotal**, never a guaranteed full journey total. Missing fares do not mean toll-free. Costs do not affect route selection.

## Current coverage

- LDP: RM 2.10 per matched plaza at Penchala, Petaling Jaya, Puchong Barat and Puchong Selatan.
- KESAS: RM 2.00 per matched charging plaza at Kemuning, Sunway and the direction-specific Awan Besar plazas.
- Curated PLUS mapping covers 63 plazas in the North–South, NKVE and ELITE network; the exact list is in `worker/toll-data.json`. This includes Seafield, USJ, Bandar Saujana Putra, Putra Heights and the Ebor plazas. Retrieve a fare only for one unambiguous ordered entry–exit pair within 1.5 km of opposite boundaries of the same reported toll section. Provider toll-section boundaries may be on access ramps, more than 1 km from the actual booths; an actual crossing within 35 m of the mapped PLUS booth is still required. Multiple or ambiguous pairs are omitted.

Fixed standard fares checked against official operator pages on 28 October 2026. Temporary discounts/exemptions are not applied. PLUS uses the existing official fare service with a shared 30-minute cache and a 6-second automatic lookup deadline. Failure leaves the fare unavailable and does not block navigation. No new paid toll service or API secret was added.

## Matching and interface

TomTom requests `sectionType=toll` alongside traffic. Matching requires a narrow actual polyline crossing, reported toll section, compatible highway identity for fixed fares, and known one-way charging direction. Reject coarse geometry and routes starting/ending at a plaza. Match selected PLUS pairs to the same section boundaries, not the nearest plaza to a user's endpoints. Repeated passes of a plaza are not fully supported.

Collapsed details show the selected route's itemised known subtotal and sources. Planning and automatic replanning replace the estimate; a replanned estimate concerns the remaining route and excludes tolls already paid. The standalone PLUS calculator remains independent. Unknown, unsupported or ambiguous routes show unavailable, never RM 0. If PLUS use is detected but cannot be priced, the breakdown and summary explicitly show the missing PLUS component. This is not nationwide coverage.

## Published-rate dataset (33 highways)

`worker/toll-rates.json` holds Class 1 car fares and plaza positions for 33 highways (5,354 fares, 227 plazas, 178 with coordinates), generated by `python3 scripts/build-toll-rates.py <toll dataset dir>` from the separate Lapan toll dataset (LLM calculator, the PLUS API and operator pages; its `SOURCES.md` lists every source). It is built into the Worker, so pricing needs no extra request and no separate server. Refreshing is: rebuild the dataset, run the script, run `npm test`, deploy.

`worker/toll-rates.js` prices a route from it **after** the careful per-operator estimate above, and never prices a highway twice (LDP, KESAS and a PLUS pair already found from the official service stay as they are).

- **TomTom decides where the route is on toll road.** A plaza is only considered if it lies within 250 m of the route along (or within 300 m of) a toll section TomTom reported. This tolerance can still match a nearby parallel road; estimates require review against official fares.
- **Closed systems** (PLUS, LPT1, WCE, ELITE, LEKAS, SKVE, SDE, EKVE): the first and last matched plaza across all toll sections are the entry and exit; if that exact pair has no published fare, a narrower published pair contributes only a partial subtotal.
- **Open systems** (LDP, KESAS, DUKE, MEX, and so on): each matched plaza is charged once at its flat fare.
- **Completeness decides the wording.** `estimate` (shown as *Toll ~RM x*) only when every toll section of 300 m or more has a priced plaza and nothing matched is left unpriced. Otherwise `partial` (*Toll RM x+*, "at least"). TomTom reporting no toll road at all gives `none` (*No toll*). A route with toll roads but no price stays `unavailable`; it is never shown as RM 0.
- **Known gaps.** 49 plazas have no coordinates (all of SKVE and EKVE, 8 of 18 on WCE, and some small urban concessions), so those highways cannot be found on a route and the label says "at least". Interchange-based concessions whose booths sit on ramps can be missed the same way. One plaza per direction is assumed for open systems; plazas that charge in one direction only are not distinguished. 14 PLUS pairs have different fares in the two directions in the dataset; they should be checked against the PLUS calculator.
- **Source confidence** (`official-api` > `official-page` > `official-form` > `official-image` > `secondary`) is kept per highway; the weakest source on a route is reported as `confidence`.

Tests: `tests/toll-rates.mjs` (data integrity, agreement with the independently checked LDP and KESAS fares, closed and open pricing, tolerance, completeness, no double charging, real fares at real plaza positions, and the full Worker path).

## Valhalla staging trial

Valhalla marks maneuvers that include mapped toll road. Lapan checks those spans against the existing Class 1 fare and plaza dataset, with the tighter crossing checks used for HERE. A match is shown as a partial subtotal. Missing or ambiguous fares remain unavailable. The staging trial does not make a live fare request.

LLM publishes plaza locations through its [GIS layer](https://gis02.llm.gov.my/llmsmpt/rest/services/GIS_LLM/PLAZA_TOL/MapServer/0). Its [public toll calculator](https://www.llm.gov.my/pages/view/) is a separate fare reference. The plaza layer returned coordinates and names in a sample query, with no fare fields. Use the calculator and official operator rates to verify changes before refreshing the bundled data.
