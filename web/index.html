About & privacy

Lapan · Version {{APP_VERSION}} · Release history ↗

Traffic-aware ETA uses TomTom when available, refreshed at most every two minutes. Otherwise a road estimate is shown. Starting and destination coordinates are sent to TomTom to plan routes using current traffic and reported closures. Reported closures are checked when a route is planned or replanned; newly blocked roads may be missing. Selected route coordinates are sent to TomTom to refresh travel time. GPS guidance is an early version; obey road signs. Toll budget is saved on this device. Searches go to Photon; additional business and address searches go to TomTom when the normal search has no matches or you select Search more places; OSRM is used only if you choose basic routing, which does not check live closures. Location is used only when you enable GPS. Quick stops sends your search location to Photon to find nearby places. Automatic replanning sends your current and destination coordinates to TomTom, or OSRM if you chose basic routing. PLUS fare requests contain only plaza codes. Anonymous visitor statistics count map page visits and active tabs, with broad device/browser categories. No names, IP addresses, GPS or routes are stored by the counter.
