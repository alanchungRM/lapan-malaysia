Your privacy

We store aggregate counts and broad device/browser types. A random browser identifier is saved locally for the current Malaysia day and rotates daily. Only a daily hash is stored on the server to deduplicate browsers and group visits. Previous-day hashes are removed on subsequent tracking requests; aggregate totals remain. Active-tab IDs expire after 90 seconds.

No names, email addresses, IP addresses, GPS coordinates, destinations or routes are stored by this visitor counter. Your browser’s Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control setting is respected. Hosting providers may keep their own operational logs.
